Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

RobloxDev Poster is an internal tool used only with TikTok accounts owned by Vshan Studios. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.

What we receive from TikTok: the basic profile of our own account (open id, display name, avatar) and the access tokens needed to upload drafts.

How we use it: only to confirm which of our accounts is connected and to upload our own video clips to its drafts.

Storage: tokens are stored on our own server, are never shared or sold, and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app.

Deletion: email us and we will delete any stored tokens within 30 days.
